\section{Discussion and Future Work}
\label{sec:limitations}
The four-system results show that PairSelect enables frozen detectors to identify anomalous frames with fewer internal tokens and no per-budget retraining. The near-dense performance at 40\% deletion in most configurations (Table~\ref{tab:quality}) provides empirical evidence that the compressed representations retain anomaly-discriminative information. Dense contextualization followed by local, magnitude-guided selection reduces internal computation while preserving the observation schedule. We report the complete fixed budget ladder to characterize this detection--efficiency trade-off across systems. These findings motivate future compression-aware WSVAD training and joint optimization of representations and selection.
