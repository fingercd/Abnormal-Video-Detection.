\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
PairSelect enables efficient frame-level anomaly detection by retaining contextualized token representatives within pretrained visual encoders. Across four encoder--detector systems on UCF-Crime and XD-Violence, the results support preservation of anomaly-discriminative information without compression-specific retraining. At 40\% nominal deletion, most configurations retain near-dense detection performance; at 60\%, encoder acceleration reaches 1.47$\times$ with generally mild performance degradation. Together, these findings demonstrate a transferable route to lower-cost visual encoding for anomaly detection while preserving input coverage.
